## About

NeoPlants-EU records established vascular plant neophytes, taxa introduced to Europe after
ca. 1492 that hold established populations in a country, at the level of a taxon in a
country. Each of the 5,744 records carries the earliest documented occurrence of that taxon
in that country, together with its life cycle, growth form, dispersal mode and native source
region on a standardised vocabulary, and whether a list for the country counts it invasive.
It covers 1,941 taxa (1,909 species in 146 families) across 54 countries and territories,
with first-record years running from 1492 to 2020.

A record enters when a vegetation plot from the European Vegetation Archive in that country
records the taxon as a neophyte. Where the country's own alien-flora catalogue grades the
invasion stage, a record that every grade calls casual is left out. The first record is the
earliest year across the Global Alien Species First Record Database, a GBIF occurrence
download, the European Vegetation Archive and national alien plant lists, and each record
names the source of its year.

I built the trait columns to carry their own provenance. 5,736 of the 5,740 recorded life
cycles, all 5,732 growth forms and 5,474 of the 5,587 dispersal modes cite a page stating
that value; the rest come from LEDA or compiled checklists, which have no per-taxon page to
cite. Names are matched against four taxonomic checklists held at fixed versions, whose
versions and checksums ship with the data, so the taxonomy can be reproduced.
